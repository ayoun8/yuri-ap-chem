5. Rate law from an elementary mechanism

The slow step on the review is:

2 ClO⁻ → ClO₂⁻ + Cl⁻   (slow)

The rate law comes from the reactants of that slow elementary step:

Rate = k[ClO⁻]²
6. Rate law from initial-rate data
Exp.[A][B]Initial rate

Compare 1 → 2. [B] triples while rate stays unchanged → zero order in B.
Compare 1 → 3. [A] doubles and rate doubles → first order in A.
Rate = k[A]; overall order = 1.
k = (0.030 M/s) / (0.10 M) = 0.30 s⁻¹

9. Zero-order integrated rate law
[A]t = [A]₀ − kt

20% complete means 80% remains:

[A]t = (0.80)(0.50 M) = 0.40 M
0.40 = 0.50 − k(10 min)
k = 0.010 M·min⁻¹
