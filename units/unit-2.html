2. Lewis structures: reliable workflow
1. Count valence e⁻
2. Pick central atom
3. Draw single bonds
4. Complete octets
5. Form multiple bonds if needed
A double bond or triple bond still counts as one electron domain when you move to VSEPR.

7. Formal charge & the better Lewis structure
formal charge = valence e⁻ − nonbonding e⁻ − ½(bonding e⁻)

In the all-single-bond sulfite structure, each O is −1 and S is +1. In the structure with one S=O double bond, the double-bonded O is 0, the two single-bonded O atoms are −1, and S is 0.

The structure with the S=O double bond is preferred because it minimizes formal charges.
