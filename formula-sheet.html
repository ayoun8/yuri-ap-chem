Gases

PV = nRT   (T in kelvin)
Boyle (n, T constant): P₁V₁ = P₂V₂
Charles (n, P constant): V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂
Gay-Lussac (n, V constant): P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂
Combined (n constant): P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂
Dalton: Ptotal = PA + PB + …     Xᵢ = nᵢ / ntotal     Pᵢ = Xᵢ Ptotal
Kinetic molecular theory
Average kinetic energy depends only on kelvin temperature. At the same T, lighter particles move faster.
Most ideal behavior
High temperature and low pressure, with weak intermolecular forces.

Solutions & Reactions

Molarity: M = mol solute / L solution
Dilution: M₁V₁ = M₂V₂
Stoichiometry: g A → mol A → mol B → g B (use the mole ratio from the balanced equation)
excess remaining = excess initial − excess used
Beer’s law: A = εbc (absorbance is proportional to concentration)
OIL RIG: oxidation is loss of electrons; reduction is gain of electrons.
